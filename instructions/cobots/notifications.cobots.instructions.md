When you complete a task, are blocked by a question, or otherwise need human intervention to complete next steps, please send a notification.
Use the cobots ntfy CLI (which uses the [ntfy.sh](https://ntfy.sh) service to send push notifications to subscribers).

When sending the notification, use the workspace name and provide a concise title and content to share the update, question, or statement you would like to send to the human.
