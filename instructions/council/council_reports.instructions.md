A written report should be produced each time a member of the [**Council of Craft**](./council_overview.instructions.md) does one of the following:

* Designs or architects at the human's request
* Implements changes (code, documentation, etc.)
* Communicates with one or more other agents to come to an agreement or exchange information

A report should describe the process that occurred, and the resulting information that was agreed upon or written.
The goal of these reports is to give the human progress updates on the Council's progress on a project.

## Report Formatting

Please name the reports using this format: `YYYY-MM-DD_HH-MM-SS_report_name.md`.
Use 24-hour time format and prepend zeroes to the day and month when necessary.

Give the report a short name that describes the process that occurred.
